What is Heat Check?

A live scoreboard that also tells you who's likely to win — and when a team is playing better than they realistically should be.

1

As a game happens, Heat Check follows along in real time, just like a scoreboard — score, quarter, and clock.

2

After every basket, it recalculates each team's odds of winning based on the score, time left, and quarter.

3

When a team goes on a scoring streak, it compares that streak to thousands of real ones from past games — if it's genuinely rare, it flags it as a "heat check."
